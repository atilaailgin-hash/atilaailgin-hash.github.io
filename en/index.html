Areas of practice

Finding the right way of working for you

Rather than applying the same method to everyone, the process takes shape around your experience and what you would like to explore today.

01

Individual sessions

One-to-one work around separation and grief, recurring relationship patterns, intense emotions, self-worth and life transitions.

  • Understanding and regulating emotions
  • Recognising thought–behaviour cycles
  • Clarifying boundaries, choices and what matters to you
02

Couples sessions

Working together to identify recurring conflict cycles, make space for unspoken feelings and build a safer emotional bond.

  • Recognising negative interaction cycles
  • Strengthening emotional accessibility
  • Developing repair and communication skills
03

Relationship coaching

Structured work to clarify relationship goals and make more deliberate choices around communication, boundaries and decision-making.

  • Defining relationship goals and expectations
  • Communication and boundary-setting skills
  • Making decision processes more concrete
Does not involve diagnosis or treatment and is not a substitute for psychotherapy.
04

Intercultural experiences

Exploring migration, belonging, cultural differences and the ways intercultural relationships shape identity and intimacy.

  • Questions of belonging and identity
  • Intercultural couple dynamics
  • Transition and adaptation

How do we work in therapy?

Choose the topic that feels closest to your experience. This short guide is not an assessment or diagnosis; it is designed to make my way of working more tangible.

01 Separation or grief

We make space for the meaning of loss and the trace it leaves.

First

We make room for the emotions stirred by the loss, the bond itself and what may still feel unfinished.

Then

Together, we identify how grief appears in everyday thought and behaviour patterns.

Aim

To create a more flexible life in which the loss is not erased, but held differently.

EFT

Helps you understand what you feel and what the emotion may be trying to tell you.

CBT

Creates practical room for change in thought and behaviour cycles that are no longer helping.

About

Psychologist Ilgın Atila, MSc

I completed my undergraduate degree in Psychology at TOBB University of Economics and Technology and my MSc in Psychology of Intercultural Relations at ISCTE in Lisbon.

My educational background enables me to understand people not only through individual characteristics, but also in the context of their relationships, culture, sense of belonging and life experiences. I bring together Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) and Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) in an integrative framework that connects emotional understanding with practical steps towards change.

My work is particularly oriented towards separation and grief, attachment needs and recurring relationship patterns. I see therapy not as a relationship in which one person gives the “right answer”, but as a space for thinking together with curiosity, openness and without judgement.
